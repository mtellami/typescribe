# Changelog

## [1.0.0](https://github.com/mtellami/typescribe/releases/tag/v1.0.0) — 2026-11-22

The first official release of Typescribe, a browser app for typing and listening
practice with YouTube videos.

- Type, Fill, Write, and Watch practice modes, with hints and selectable typing density.
- Video previews, caption imports, a shared library, recent videos, and shareable practice links.
- Email/password and Google sign-in, profile settings, and password updates.
- Live WPM and accuracy, caption replay, playback speed, and session summaries.
- Selectable layouts, preset and custom Google Fonts, and key and error sounds.
- Persistent browser preferences, loading skeletons, and responsive controls.
- Editable word boundaries and incorrect spaces that can be corrected with Backspace.
- Standalone app structure with PostgreSQL, Docker Compose, Make commands, and Vercel configuration.
